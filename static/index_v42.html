      how it works

      Three nets.
      One shield.

      Any single detector can be beaten. A call must pass all three nets — voice, words, identity — before the shield stays calm.

      Voice DNA

      A convolutional engine reads the spectral fingerprint of the voice — synthetic speech, clones and robocall delivery light it up. Trained on 121,461 benchmark clips.

      mel-cnn · 236k params

      Scam radar

      Fraud calls follow scripts. The radar hears the words — digital arrest, OTP, fake emergencies, manufactured urgency — in Hindi, English and Hinglish.

      live transcript + text check

      Family voiceprint

      With consent, family members enrol once. When a call claims to be Papa, the shield asks: does this voice match Papa — or someone else entirely?

      on-device · consent-first
      Safe
      No synthetic indicators. The call flows normally.
      Warn
      Possible indicators. Ask the code word. Call back on the saved number.
      Hold
      Strong sustained evidence. The transfer is paused until a human confirms out-of-band.
